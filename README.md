## ✨ About THEGCTC

**THEGCTC** is a modern online home decor store focused on stylish, functional, and carefully selected decorative pieces.

Our goal is to help customers transform ordinary spaces into beautiful, comfortable, and personalized environments.

From elegant wall decorations and lighting to decorative accessories, candles, mirrors, cushions, and seasonal pieces, THEGCTC offers products for every room and style.

---

## 🏠 Our Mission

Our mission is simple:

> To make beautiful home decoration accessible, inspiring, and enjoyable.

We believe every home should reflect the personality of the people who live in it.

THEGCTC combines modern design, timeless pieces, practical accessories, and decorative details to help customers create spaces they truly love.
